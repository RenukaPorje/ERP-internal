# Incubyte ERP Core: Document Roadmap

We are replacing the in-house HR tool (built on Frappe Cloud) with a new internal ERP core for people work. We work through the documents **one stage at a time**, from high level to detailed. Each stage is approved before the next one starts.

| Stage | Question it answers | Folder | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Problem & Vision** | Why are we doing this, and what does success look like? | [stage-1-problem-and-vision](stage-1-problem-and-vision/) | Drafted, awaiting review |
| **2. Discovery** | How does the current tool and process work today? | [stage-2-discovery](stage-2-discovery/) | Drafted; interviews 12–19 Nov |
| **3. Product Definition** | What are we building? (module map, high-level requirements, PRD) | [stage-3-product-definition](stage-3-product-definition/) | Drafted, in line with Stages 1–2; final pass after the interviews |
| 4. Requirements | What exactly must each module do? | [later-stages/stage-4-requirements](later-stages/stage-4-requirements/) | Early draft, parked |
| 5. Experience Design | How will it look and feel? | — | Later |
| 6. Technical Design | How is it built? | [later-stages/stage-6-technical-design](later-stages/stage-6-technical-design/) | Early draft, parked |
| 7. Detailed Design | How is each piece built? | — | Later |
| 8. Delivery Readiness | How do we ship it safely? | — | Later |

**Current goal:** complete and approve Stages 1–3 by about **24 Nov 2026** (Stage 2 interviews 12–19 Nov).

**Approvers:** the Founders and the Operations Head. **Consulted:** HR / People Success, Finance, IT / Security.

**Modules by release:**
- **Release 1:** Employee Profile, Leaves, Expenses, and the reports used today
- **Release 2:** Feedback & 360 Reviews, PIP, Onboarding & Offboarding, Letters
- **Release 3:** Policies & Announcements, Teams & Pods, Assignments
- Microsoft Teams integration where useful

**How we review:** each document ends with `[ ] Reviewed`. Mark it `[x] Reviewed` once it's agreed. Add `@bee` comments anywhere to request changes.
