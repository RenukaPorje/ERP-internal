# Vision and Scope

## The problem

Incubyte runs its people work (profiles, leave, expenses, feedback and more) on an in-house tool built on Frappe Cloud. It is hard to use, dated, slow, sometimes wrong, missing features we need, and it shows people information beyond what their role should allow. It also holds our most sensitive employee data.

The employee survey (Nov 2026) came back with **mostly low satisfaction** *(exact score to be added)*. Themes, starting with the most raised:

| Theme | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Security** | People can see colleagues' information beyond what their role should allow, so they don't trust the tool with their data |
| **Usability** | Simple tasks take too many steps, so people ask HR instead |
| **UI/UX design** | Screens are built around data tables, not the job at hand. Menus show options people can't use. |
| **Functionality** | Some features are incorrect, and we need new ones (for example policies and announcements, and self-service letters) |
| **Performance** | The tool is slow |

**Who it affects:** employees find it inefficient in everyday tasks, find it hard to trust the tool with their sensitive data and files; managers and pod leads find approvals and team information hard to act on; HR / People Success answer questions and fix data the tool should handle.

**Why now:** security is the top complaint, the problems are in the tool's foundations rather than individual features, and we're small enough to move cleanly (fewer than 300 people, all on Microsoft 365). Doing nothing means continued distrust, lost time every week, and more workarounds.

## Vision

**One secure, simple and fast home for everyday people work at Incubyte: employees trust it with their data and files and get tasks done themselves, managers act on approvals and team information easily, and HR / People Success stop doing the tool's job. Built on the Microsoft 365 tools we already use.**

## Goals

Each goal answers one of the problems above.

| Goal | Answers | In practice |
|---|---|---|
| **Secure by default** | Security | Microsoft sign-in. People see only what their role needs. Colleagues' leave reasons, expenses, personal details and files stay private. |
| **Simple to use** | Usability | Common tasks take a few clear steps, so employees do them themselves instead of asking HR |
| **Designed around the job** | UI/UX design | Screens follow the task, not data tables. Menus show only what you can use, and nothing blocks your way. |
| **Correct and complete** | Functionality | Features work correctly, and data is right. Fills today's gaps: policies and announcements, self-service letters, 360-degree reviews, PIP |
| **Fast** | Performance | Common screens load quickly on laptop or phone |
| **Leave Frappe cleanly** | Why now | Move our data across, check it, and switch the old tool off |

## Modules by release

| Release | Modules |
|---|---|
| **1: Core** | Employee Profile, Leaves, Expenses, with Microsoft sign-in, role-based access, audit trail, notifications and the data move. Includes the reports used today: Leave Balance, Leave Balance Summary and Vehicle Expenses. |
| **2: Performance and lifecycle** | Feedback and 360 reviews, PIP, Onboarding & Offboarding, Letters |
| **3: Organisation and communication** | Policies & Announcements (portal, email and a company-wide Teams channel), Teams & Pods, Assignments |
| **Later** | Wider reports and dashboards |

**Out of scope:** payroll, accounting, recruitment, bench planning, mobile apps.

## Principles

- **Security first:** a launch requirement, not a later phase.
- **Designed around user journeys:** screens follow core user workflows, not data tables.
- **Microsoft 365 native:** sign-in, Outlook and Teams.
- **Only what we need:** leave payroll and accounting to the systems built for them.
