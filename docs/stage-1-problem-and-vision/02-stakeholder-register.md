# Stakeholder Register

## Who's involved

| Who | Role in this work | What they need |
|---|---|---|
| **Founders** | Sponsors, approve | Confidence in direction, risk and investment |
| **Operations Head** | Sponsor, approves | A clear plan and a reliable way to reach everyone |
| **People Success Team (PST)** | Consulted, main users | Correct data and less manual work |
| **HR** | Consulted, main users | Correct records and fewer questions to answer |
| **Talent Acquisition (TA)** | Consulted | A smooth, complete onboarding |
| **Finance** | Consulted | Clean, approved expense claims |
| **IT / Security** | Consulted | Secure sign-in and controlled access |
| **Managers, pod leads, employees** | Interviewed, kept informed | A simple tool that just works |
| **Product / build team** | Does the work | Clear decisions to build on |

## Approvals

Every stage is approved by the **Founders and the Operations Head**, after input from PST, HR, TA, Finance and IT / Security.

| Stage | Documents | What gets approved |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Problem and vision** | Vision and Scope, Stakeholder Register, Options Analysis | The problem, vision, goals, release plan, the approval process, and the build vs buy decision |
| **2. Discovery** | As-Is Assessment, Discovery Plan | How things work today, and what to keep, fix or drop |
| **3. Product definition** | PRD | What each module does, who sees what, the data move, and answers to the open questions (or who will answer them) |
