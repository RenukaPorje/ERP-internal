# Problem Statement

*Stage 1 · For leadership review · Draft*

## The problem

Incubyte runs its people work (profiles, leave, expenses, feedback and more) on an in-house tool built on Frappe Cloud. It is hard to use, dated, slow, sometimes wrong, missing features we need, and it shows people information beyond what their role should allow. It also holds our most sensitive employee data.

**We need one secure, easy-to-use place for everyday people work that everyone can trust.**

## What employees told us

The employee survey (Nov 2026) came back with **mostly low satisfaction** *(exact score to be added)*. Themes, starting with the most raised:

| Theme | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Security** | People can see colleagues' information beyond what their role should allow, so they don't trust the tool with their data |
| **Usability** | Simple tasks take too many steps, so people ask HR instead |
| **UI/UX design** | Screens are built around data tables, not the job at hand. Menus show options people can't use. |
| **Functionality** | Some features behave incorrectly, and some are missing (for example policies and announcements, and self-service letters) |
| **Performance** | The tool is slow |

## Who it affects

- **Employees:** everyday tasks take longer than they should.
- **Managers and pod leads:** approvals and team information are hard to act on.
- **HR / People Success:** they answer questions and fix data the tool should handle.
- **Finance:** expense handling isn't as clean or reliable as needed.
- **Leadership:** carries the risk and the cost of a tool that slows people down.

## Cost of doing nothing

- People keep not trusting the tool with their personal data.
- Time is lost every week across employees, managers, HR and Finance.
- Spreadsheets, emails and chats keep replacing the tool.
- Each new need becomes another workaround.

## Why now

- **Security is the top complaint** in the survey.
- **Patching has limits:** the problems are in the foundations, not just in individual features.
- **We're small enough to move cleanly:** fewer than 300 people, all on Microsoft 365.
