# Build vs Buy

*Stage 1 · For leadership review · Draft*

**This is a leadership decision.** These are the options the team considered, with the team's leaning.

## Options

| Option | What it means |
|---|---|
| **1. Rebuild from scratch** | Our own web app, designed around our workflows |
| **2. Build on Frappe HR** | Stay on Frappe and rework our customisations on its standard HR product |
| **3. Buy, build the gaps** | Subscribe to a ready-made HR product (for example Keka, Zoho People, Darwinbox) and build what's missing |
| **4. Hybrid** | Buy the standard parts (leave, records) and build the Incubyte-specific parts (pods, assignments, feedback, announcements) |

## How they compare

| | 1. Rebuild | 2. Frappe HR | 3. Buy | 4. Hybrid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fit to how we work** | Highest | Medium | Lower | Medium to high |
| **User experience** | In our control | Similar to today | Good, but generic | Two experiences |
| **Security** | In our control | Same platform as today | With the vendor | Split |
| **Time to first value** | Slower | Faster | Fastest | Medium |
| **Effort** | High | Low to medium | Low to medium | Medium |
| **Main risk** | Needs a build team, and takes longer | Repeats today's problems | Doesn't fit how we work | Two systems to keep in step |

## Team's leaning: Option 1, rebuild

- Today's problems come from the foundations. Options 2 and 4 keep some of them.
- Several modules are specific to Incubyte (pods, assignments, 360 reviews, Teams announcements).
- Security and Microsoft 365 are built in from the start.

**Trade-off:** it's slower than buying. If speed matters more than fit, Option 3 or 4 becomes more attractive.

## Decisions needed from leadership

1. Choose an option.
2. Decide whether to get prices or run a short trial of a ready-made product first.
3. Decide who builds it, and roughly when.

*There's no hard deadline to switch off Frappe. It goes when the new platform is ready.*
