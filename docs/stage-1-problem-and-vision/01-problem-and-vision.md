# Problem and Vision

*Stage 1 · For leadership review · Draft*

## The problem

Incubyte runs its people work (profiles, leave, expenses, feedback and more) on an in-house tool built on Frappe Cloud. It is hard to use, dated, slow, sometimes wrong, missing features we need, and it shows people information beyond what their role should allow. It also holds our most sensitive employee data.

The employee survey (Nov 2026) came back with **mostly low satisfaction** *(exact score to be added)*. Themes, starting with the most raised:

| Theme | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Security** | People can see colleagues' information beyond what their role should allow, so they don't trust the tool with their data |
| **Usability** | Simple tasks take too many steps, so people ask HR instead |
| **UI/UX design** | Screens are built around data tables, not the job at hand. Menus show options people can't use. |
| **Functionality** | Some features behave incorrectly, and some are missing (for example policies and announcements, and self-service letters) |
| **Performance** | The tool is slow |

**Who it affects:** employees lose time on everyday tasks; managers and pod leads find approvals and team information hard to act on; HR / People Success answer questions and fix data the tool should handle; Finance can't rely on expense handling.

**Why now:** security is the top complaint, the problems are in the tool's foundations rather than individual features, and we're small enough to move cleanly (fewer than 300 people, all on Microsoft 365). Doing nothing means continued distrust, lost time every week, and more spreadsheets and workarounds.

## Vision

**One secure, simple home for everyday people work at Incubyte, built around how we work and the Microsoft 365 tools we already use.**

## Goals

| Goal | In practice |
|---|---|
| **Secure by default** | Microsoft sign-in. People see only what their role needs. No one sees a colleague's leave reasons, expenses or personal details without a reason. |
| **Easy to use and fast** | Common tasks take a few clear steps on laptop or phone. Nothing blocks your way, and menus show only what you can use. |
| **Data people trust** | Leave balances, records and team information are correct |
| **Fill today's gaps** | Policies and announcements, self-service letters, 360-degree reviews |
| **Leave Frappe cleanly** | Move our data across, check it, and switch the old tool off |

## Success measures

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Employee satisfaction with the tool | Clear improvement on today's mostly-low score *(proposed: +30 points)* |
| People seeing colleagues' information beyond their role | None |
| Serious security issues at launch | None |
| Page load for common screens | Under 2 seconds |
| HR questions the tool should answer | Halved within 3 months *(proposed)* |
| Leave balances correct after the move | 100%, signed off by HR |
| Old Frappe tool | Switched off |

## Modules by release

| Release | Modules |
|---|---|
| **1: Core** | Employee Profile, Leaves, Expenses, with Microsoft sign-in, role-based access, audit trail, notifications and the data move. Includes the reports used today: Leave Balance, Leave Balance Summary and Vehicle Expenses. |
| **2: Performance and lifecycle** | Feedback and 360 reviews, PIP, Onboarding & Offboarding, Letters |
| **3: Organisation and communication** | Policies & Announcements (portal, email and a company-wide Teams channel), Teams & Pods, Assignments |
| **Later** | Wider reports and dashboards |

**Out of scope:** payroll, accounting, recruitment, bench planning, mobile apps.

## Principles

- **Security first:** a launch requirement, not a later phase.
- **Designed around tasks**, not data tables.
- **Microsoft 365 native:** sign-in, Outlook and Teams.
- **Only what we need:** leave payroll and accounting to the systems built for them.
