# Stakeholders and Approvals

*Stage 1 · For leadership review · Draft*

## Who's involved

| Who | Role in this work | What they need |
|---|---|---|
| **Founders** | Sponsors, approve | Confidence in direction, risk and investment |
| **Operations Head** | Sponsor, approves | A clear plan and a reliable way to reach everyone |
| **HR / People Success** | Consulted, main users | Correct data and less manual work |
| **Finance** | Consulted | Clean, approved expense claims |
| **IT / Security** | Consulted | Secure sign-in and controlled access |
| **Managers, pod leads, employees** | Interviewed, kept informed | A simple tool that just works |
| **Product / build team** | Does the work | Clear decisions to build on |

## Approvals

Every stage is approved by the **Founders and the Operations Head**, after input from HR / People Success, Finance and IT / Security.

| Stage | What gets approved |
|---|---|
| **1. Problem and vision** | Problem, vision, goals, release plan, build vs buy decision |
| **2. Discovery** | How things work today, the keep/fix/add/drop list, the data approach |
| **3. Product definition** | Module map, high-level requirements, PRD |
