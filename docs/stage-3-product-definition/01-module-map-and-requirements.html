Who uses it

One person can hold several roles. A manager is also an employee, and a pod lead may also be a manager.

Business modules

The core functionality of each module, in plain terms.

01

Employee Profile & Directory

Release 1

A single, trusted record of every employee.

  • Employees view their profile and update their own personal and contact details.
  • HR creates and maintains employee records: job title, department, location, manager, joining date and employment type.
  • Every change to someone's role, manager or location is kept as history.
  • Anyone can search the directory and see who reports to whom in the org chart.
  • Salary, ID numbers and bank details are protected. Only the employee and HR can see them.
  • Employee documents (ID proofs, certificates) are stored securely.
Used byEmployeeHRManager
02

Leaves & Holidays

Release 1

Apply for leave, get it approved, and always know your balance.

  • Employees apply for leave by choosing type, dates (full or half day) and a reason.
  • The People Success Team approves or rejects, as today. PST also handles long or emergency leave for employees on probation.
  • Employees can cancel or withdraw a request.
  • Each employee sees their balance per leave type (available, used, pending) and its history.
  • HR sets up leave types and their rules: Earned, Casual, Sick, Birthday, Bereavement, Unpaid and others.
  • Leave builds up, carries forward and lapses automatically. The leave year runs January to December.
  • HR maintains holiday calendars per location (fixed, optional and floating). Employees pick their optional holidays.
  • A team calendar shows who is away, without leave reasons.
  • Everyone involved is notified at each step.
Used byEmployeePeople SuccessHRManager
03

Expenses

Release 1

Claim work expenses easily and get them to Finance for payout.

  • Employees create a claim with one or more items, each with date, category (including vehicle expenses), amount and a receipt upload.
  • Employees see the status of each claim: draft, submitted, returned, approved, rejected or exported.
  • Finance approves, rejects, or returns a claim for changes. Only the claimant and Finance see a claim.
  • Finance sets up expense categories and spending limits. Items over the limit need a justification.
  • Likely duplicate receipts are flagged.
  • Finance exports approved claims to the finance/payroll system for payment. Each claim is exported only once.
Used byEmployeeFinance
04

Feedback & Reviews

Release 2Confidential

A culture of continuous feedback, plus structured periodic reviews.

  • Anyone can give feedback to a colleague at any time.
  • Recipients see the feedback they've been given, and can choose to share an item with their manager.
  • HR runs 360 review cycles, deciding who is included, which questions are asked, and the deadlines.
  • Each person writes a self-review and nominates peers. The nominated peers write reviews, and the manager writes the final review.
  • Employees read and acknowledge their finalised review.
  • Reminders go out automatically before deadlines, and HR tracks completion.
  • Reviews and feedback are private to the people involved.
Used byEmployeeManagerHR
05

PIP

Release 2Confidential

A fair, structured and confidential way to support someone who needs to improve.

  • HR and the manager create a performance improvement plan with goals, success criteria, duration and check-in dates.
  • The employee sees the plan and acknowledges it.
  • At each check-in, the manager records progress against each goal, and the employee can comment.
  • The outcome is recorded as Successful, Extended or Unsuccessful.
  • Only the employee, their manager and HR can see a PIP. It never appears anywhere else.
Used byHRManagerEmployee
06

Assignments

Release 3

Kept from today's tool. Its purpose is confirmed in the Stage 2 interviews before it is designed; the points below are a starting idea.

  • HR or delivery maintains the list of clients and projects. It's reference data only, with no billing.
  • People are assigned to projects with start and end dates and an allocation %. Total allocation can't go over 100%.
  • Employees see their current and past assignments. Managers and pod leads see their team's.
  • Assignments end automatically when someone leaves.
Used byHREmployeeManagerPod Lead
07

Teams / Pods

Release 3

Show how Incubyte is organised into pods.

  • HR creates pods and assigns members and a pod lead.
  • Employees see their pod, its lead and their pod-mates.
  • Pod leads see their pod's members, assignments and who is on leave.
  • Pod moves are kept as history.
Used byHRPod LeadEmployee
08

Onboarding & Offboarding

Release 2

A smooth first day and a clean exit.

  • HR or People Success define checklist templates for joining and exit.
  • Adding a new employee starts their onboarding checklist, with tasks for TA, HR, People Success, the manager, the buddy and the joiner.
  • Joiners upload documents and complete their tasks before and after day 1.
  • When HR starts an exit, an offboarding checklist is created covering asset return, handover and access removal.
  • Progress is tracked, and overdue tasks trigger reminders.
  • Assets issued at joining are tracked and recovered at exit.
Used byPeople SuccessTAHRManagerBuddy
09

Policies & Announcements

Release 3

One official place for company policies and news, with a record of every communication.

  • HR publishes policies by category and location, with version history.
  • Employees acknowledge mandatory policies. HR tracks who has, and reminders go to those who haven't.
  • HR, Admin or Operations post announcements to everyone, or to chosen locations, departments or pods. They can be pinned and given an expiry date.
  • Every new policy, policy update and announcement goes out in three places at once.
1 · PortalNotification and the Policies & Announcements page
2 · EmailTo every employee in the audience
3 · Microsoft TeamsPost in the company channel, under the publisher's own name
  • Each item keeps a permanent broadcast record of when it was published, by whom, and where it went, with delivery times. Failed deliveries are retried and can be resent.
Used byHROperationsAdminEmployee
10

Letters & Self-Service Documents

Release 2

Employees get standard HR letters without waiting on HR.

  • HR maintains letter templates: employment verification, address proof, salary certificate and experience letter.
  • Employees request a letter and download it as a PDF. Some types, such as the salary certificate, need HR approval first.
  • Every letter gets a reference number, is stored and is audited. Salary letters are visible only to the employee and HR.
Used byEmployeeHR

Platform modules

Shared services every business module relies on.

11

Access & Security

Release 1
  • Sign in with the Incubyte Microsoft account only. There are no separate passwords.
  • Each person sees only what their role and relationship allow: self, team, pod or everyone.
  • Sensitive and confidential data is encrypted and hidden from anyone not entitled to it.
  • Sessions time out automatically, and exited employees lose access.
12

Notifications

Release 1
  • Email (Microsoft 365) and in-app notifications for approvals, decisions, tasks and reminders.
  • Policy and announcement broadcasts also post to a Microsoft Teams channel.
  • Notifications never contain sensitive or confidential details.
13

Audit Trail

Release 1
  • Records who did what and when, for every change and every view of sensitive or confidential data.
  • It can't be edited or deleted. Admin and HR can search it.
14

Reports

Release 1Later
  • Release 1: the reports used today, namely Leave Balance, Leave Balance Summary and Vehicle Expenses.
  • Later: wider reports and dashboards, such as headcount, attrition and review completion.
  • The same access rules apply. PIPs are never included.
15

Data Migration

Release 1
  • Brings people records, leave balances and all leave history, past expense claims with receipts, and pods over from the current Frappe tool. Holidays are set up fresh.
  • Produces a reconciliation report for HR to sign off before switching over.

Cross-cutting requirements

AreaRequirement
UsabilityEvery common task takes a few clicks. Works on desktop and phone browsers. Accessible to WCAG 2.2 AA.
SecurityMicrosoft sign-in only, role-based access, encrypted sensitive data, and a security review before launch
PrivacyPeople see only what their role needs. Private details, expenses and performance records stay with the people involved and HR. Data hosted in India.
ReliabilityDaily backups, about 99.5% availability, and no lost or duplicated leave or expense records
PerformancePages load in under 2 seconds for up to 1,000 employees

  • Recruitment / applicant tracking
  • Bench, roll-off forecasting and staffing search
  • Timesheets
  • Mobile apps, and interactive Teams or Slack apps (posting broadcasts to a Teams channel is included)
