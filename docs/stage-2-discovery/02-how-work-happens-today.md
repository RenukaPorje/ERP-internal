# Today's Journeys

*Stage 2 · For leadership review · Draft*

How the Release 1 tasks work today, to be checked in walkthroughs.

## Apply for and approve leave

1. The employee checks their balance and applies in the tool.
2. The People Success Team (PST) approves or rejects. PST also steps in for long or emergency leave for employees on probation.
3. The employee sees the outcome and their new balance.

**Pain:**
- Slow, and not intuitive. Hard to use or follow.
- Employees can see beyond their role. Personal and sensitive information and files are visible to other employees in some places.
- Poor usability, UI and UX, reflected in low survey satisfaction.

## Submit and approve an expense

1. The employee creates a claim and attaches receipts.
2. Finance approves, rejects or sends it back.
3. Finance pays out approved claims outside the tool.

**Pain:**
- Hard to use, slow, and not intuitive.
- Employees can see beyond their role. Personal and sensitive information and files are visible to other employees in some places.
- Poor usability, UI and UX, reflected in low survey satisfaction.

## Update my profile

1. The employee edits the details they're allowed to change.
2. HR makes other changes on request.

**Pain:** colleagues can see more than they should, and it's hard to use.
