## Access and privacy principles

1. **See only what your role needs.**
2. **Private stays private:** leave reasons, expenses and personal details are visible only to the person, their approvers and HR.
3. **Performance records** (reviews, PIPs) are seen only by the people involved and HR.
4. **Menus match the person:** you see only what you can use.
5. **Nothing forces itself on you:** prompts can always be dismissed.
6. **Sensitive access is recorded.**
7. **One Microsoft sign-in.** Leavers lose access straight away.
