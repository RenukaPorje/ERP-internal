# Discovery Plan

Short interviews (30–45 minutes), with a walkthrough of real tasks where useful. They fill the gaps in the [as-is assessment](01-as-is-assessment.md).

## Who we ask, and what

**People Success Team (PST) and HR** (one session, both together)
- Which work does PST own and which does HR? In particular: leave decisions, special leave cases, offboarding, employee records, letters and policies.
- Who runs offboarding: PST or HR? Who starts an exit, who owns the checklist, and who closes it?
- Which profile details can employees change themselves, and which go through HR?
- With unlimited leave, what does a "balance" track, and which leave types are still used?
- How are quorum, black-out dates and probation limits checked today?
- Is it right that the pod lead approves, then PST decides? Which cases go to HR: leave during probation (including long or emergency leave), maternity leave, paternity leave, and possibly leave of absence?
- How do feedback, 360 reviews and PIPs run today, if at all, and in the tool or outside it?
- Which letters are requested most? Who uses the leave balance reports?

**Talent Acquisition (TA)** *(interviewed; notes to be added)*
- How does onboarding run today, from offer acceptance to the joiner settling in?
- Which steps happen in the tool, and which in email or spreadsheets?
- Which documents are collected from joiners, and who verifies them?
- How are the buddy and the bi-weekly 1:1 set up?
- What do joiners need to do before Day 1, using their personal email access?

**Finance**
- What are the limits and receipt rules for each expense category?
- Is approval by Finance only? How do approved claims get paid?
- Who uses the Vehicle Expenses report?

**IT / Security**
- Who should see what?
- How do sign-in and leaver access work?
- Joiners get access through their personal email before Day 1. Can that access be limited to the onboarding module only?
- What should the Teams integration cover?

**Managers and pod leads**
- **What is Assignments for?**
- What should "Teams & Pods" be called? Is your pod lead your manager?
- How does your team set black-out dates and count quorum?

**Employees (4–6)**
- Walk us through leave, expenses and profile. What slows you down?
- What do you do outside the tool?
- Where do you find policies and announcements today?
