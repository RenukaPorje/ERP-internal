# Discovery Plan

*Stage 2 · For leadership review · Draft*

Short interviews (30–45 minutes), with a walkthrough of real tasks where useful.

## Who we ask, and what

| Group | Key questions |
|---|---|
| **HR / People Success** | Which leave types and rules are used, including the probation rule? Should managers take part in leave approval? How do feedback, 360 reviews, PIPs and onboarding run? Which letters are requested? Who uses the leave balance reports? |
| **Finance** | Which expense categories and limits apply, including vehicle expenses? Is approval by Finance only? How do approved claims get paid? |
| **IT / Security** | Who should see what? How do sign-in and leaver access work? What should the Teams integration cover? |
| **Managers and pod leads** | **What is Assignments for?** What should "Teams & Pods" be called? Is your pod lead your manager? |
| **Employees (4–6)** | Walk us through leave, expenses and profile. What slows you down? What do you do outside the tool? |
